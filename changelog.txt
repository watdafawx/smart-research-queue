Version: 1.4.0
Date: 2026-10-12
  Changes:
    - Auto Research's drawer and the settings sit on the window's edges again, also after "Reset size and place", a
      middle click on the title bar or a change of screen size or GUI scale (they were drawn over the window).
    - Auto Research's toggle is a science flask, lit while its panel shows; its tooltip says so, and whether auto
      research is on.
    - The settings are headed with the mod's name and say what every mod's settings say (fse-std's texts: Docked,
      Theme..., "Reset size and place" for "Centre it"). They end on a seam between the technology slots, not across one.
    - The queue's panel is "Queue" (the window is "Research queue").
    - Docked, the Auto Research drawer runs from the window's panels' top to their bottom, on every theme.
    - Goals and blacklist in columns: names one width, the move and remove buttons lined up under each other.
    - "Filter the list to these packs" (fits on monospace themes); an empty queue keeps no room for a scroll bar.
    - Goals: tool-sized move and remove buttons; the first goal has no "up", the last no "down".
    - The cost sits beside the technology, under its description; an empty queue takes one row, with a hint.
    - Every text of the settings and questions can be translated (locale).
    - On themes with dark buttons, the queue's and goals' tool icons and the graph button's are white.
    - Needs fse-std 0.7.0+ for its themes (optional as before).

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-02
  Features:
    - Fork of Ultimate Research Queue for 2.x 2.0.9 with Auto Research built in (side panel)
    - Auto research picks from goals first, then by strategy (balanced, fast, slow, cheap, expensive, random)
    - Allowed science packs, optionally only packs made in the last hour; filter the technology list by them
    - Goals and blacklist, managed from the technology details and the side panel
    - Allow switching, goals only, infinite technologies last, announce completed research
    - Default goals and blacklist from per-player settings; Auto Research's remote interface
  Bugfixes:
    - Crash opening technologies that unlock space platforms (nil effect button)
    - Double-click to queue often missed (window was 20 ticks; now 30 = Windows' default)
    - Multi-level queue entries reset to the first level after a mod update
    - Negative time estimates right after the research changed
    - "Requires player action" repeated on every update; trigger technologies now stay queued while the
      labs work on the next startable entry
    - Queue limit raised from 49 to 280 (deep prerequisite chains in overhauls)
    - Removed the unused "print message on research completed" setting (now a panel option)
